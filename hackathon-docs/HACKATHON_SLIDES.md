# 📊 Gaddhamukt — Hackathon Presentation Slides (10-Slide Deck)

---

## Slide 1: Title & Vision
### **GADDHAMUKT (गड्ढामुक्त)**
#### *Report Potholes. Fix Roads. Hold Engineers Accountable.*

- **Vision**: India’s First Open Civic Road Infrastructure & Defect Liability Platform.
- **Presenter**: Sushant Chand & Team
- **Test Corridor**: Punjab LPU GT Road (NH-44 Phagwara) & Urban Bengaluru (BBMP)

---

## Slide 2: The Problem Statement
### **Why Indian Roads Suffer Constant Potholes & Zero Accountability**

1. 🕳️ **Fatal Road Hazards**: Thousands of annual motorcycle accidents caused by unmonitored asphalt craters.
2. ❓ **Jurisdiction Ambiguity**: Citizens don't know whether a road belongs to PWD, NHAI, BBMP, or local Gram Panchayat.
3. 🕳️ **Blackhole Complaints**: Traditional portals absorb grievances with zero SLA tracking or proof of physical repair.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
### **Gaddhamukt: Closed-Loop Civic Resolution Engine**

- 📸 **Photo Geo-Tagging**: Instant GPS location pin & reverse geocoding.
- ⚡ **72-Hour Statutory SLA Engine**: Dynamic countdown based on hazard severity.
- ↔️ **Before & After Repair Proof**: Visual compaction roller inspection sign-off.
- 📜 **Automated RTI & CMO Escalation**: Pre-formatted legal dossiers generated in 1-click.

---

## Slide 4: Core Unique Features
### **Built to Wow Citizens & Enforce Contractor Liability**

- 🛠️ **Tubelight Active Navigation**: Smooth sliding lamp glow tab transitions.
- ↔️ **Interactive Before/After Drag Slider**: Dual-layer asphalt comparison.
- 🌲 **Alternating Step Timeline**: Step 1 Left, Step 2 Right scroll-triggered audit tree.
- 📄 **Instant PDF Generator**: Exports official Section 72 Municipal Gazette dossiers via `jspdf`.

---

## Slide 5: Dual Urban & Rural Coverage
### **Tailored for Municipal Corporations & Gram Panchayats**

- 🏙️ **Urban Local Bodies (ULB)**: Ward Engineers, BBMP Infrastructure Cell, Traffic Police.
- 🌾 **Rural Panchayati Raj (PRD)**: Gram Panchayat Sarpanchs, BDO Offices, PMGSY Road Contractors.
- 📍 **Punjab LPU Live Corridor**: GT Road Phagwara Gate 1, Law Gate Market, and Chaheru Panchayat link roads.

---

## Slide 6: System Architecture & Technology Stack
### **High-Performance Modern Web Platform**

- ⚡ **Core Stack**: Vite 6, React 19, TypeScript 5.8, TailwindCSS v4.
- 🎨 **Animations**: `motion/react` (Framer Motion) GPU-accelerated micro-interactions.
- 🗺️ **Geospatial Engine**: Leaflet maps with custom SVG markers.
- 💾 **State Persistence**: Client-side LocalStorage hook engine (`useIssues`).

---

## Slide 7: Public Governance Dashboard
### **Open Data & Real-Time Performance Benchmarks**

- 📊 **6 KPI Metrics**: Total Hazards, Confirmations, Active Crews, Resolved, Overdue, Escalated.
- ⚡ **Service Speed Benchmarks**: ~14h Municipal Acknowledgement / ~2.8 Days Physical Resolution.
- 📥 **Open Data Export**: Download raw JSON datasets for public research.

---

## Slide 8: Municipal Officer Dispatch Console
### **Linear-Style Bespoke Work Order Desk**

- 🛠️ **High-Density Work Order Queue**: Filter by Punjab LPU, Urban, or Rural regions.
- 🚨 **Emergency SLA Overdue Flags**: Highlight breached statutory response deadlines.
- 📸 **Proof Upload Modal**: Municipal officers attach site compaction photos for citizen sign-off.

---

## Slide 9: Scalability & Public Impact
### **Why Gaddhamukt Wins**

- 🚀 **100% Serverless & Lightning Fast**: Zero backend overhead, instant page loads.
