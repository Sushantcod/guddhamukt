GADDHAMUKT
(गड्ढामुक्त)

Report Potholes. Fix Roads. Hold Engineers Accountable.

Presented by: Sushant Chand • Corridor: Punjab LPU GT Road & BBMP
THE CORE PROBLEM

Why Indian Roads Have Non-Stop Potholes

🕳️

Fatal Road Hazards

Thousands of commuters face severe accidents every monsoon due to unmonitored asphalt craters and deep road depressions.

❓

Jurisdiction Confusion

Citizens don't know whether a road belongs to PWD, NHAI, Municipal Corporation, or Gram Panchayat.

📜

Zero Liability

Contractors face no penalties for sub-standard asphalt compaction. Complaints disappear into blackhole portals.

THE SOLUTION

Gaddhamukt Closed-Loop Resolution Engine

1. Photo Geo-Tagging & Ward Auto-Routing

Citizens upload photos with instant GPS location mapping to auto-assign the exact Ward Engineer or Panchayat Sarpanch.

2. Statutory 72-Hour SLA Countdown

Dynamic resolution countdown timer (24h Immediate Danger, 48h High, 72h Medium) under Municipal Gazette guidelines.

3. 1-Click RTI & CMO PDF Dossier Generator

If an SLA is breached, citizens generate a formatted legal PDF complaint packet to submit directly to the Chief Minister's Office.

4. Interactive Before & After Repair Proof

Dual-layer drag slider allowing citizens to visually verify asphalt compaction rollers and completed resurfacing.
