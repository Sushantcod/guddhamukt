## 🚀 Master 1-Click Prompt for Gamma.app / Canva AI / SlidesAI

Copy-paste the prompt below into **Gamma.app** (Create with AI &rarr; Generate Slide Deck) or **Canva AI / Pitch.com**:

```text
Create a high-impact 6-slide Smart India Hackathon (SIH) presentation deck for a civic tech software project called "Gaddhamukt (गड्ढामुक्त)". 

Theme: Dark Navy (#07172B) background, Vibrant Civic Orange (#F97316) highlights, and crisp high-contrast white typography. Include point-by-point bullet lists and clear visual workflow cards.

Slide 1: Title & Problem Statement Header
- Problem Statement ID: SIH-2025-CIVIC-042
- Title: Automated Road Pothole Detection, Municipal SLA Tracking & Contractor Liability Platform
- Solution Name: GADDHAMUKT (गड्ढामुक्त) — Report Potholes. Fix Roads. Hold Engineers Accountable.
- Team Leader: Sushant Chand (Lovely Professional University - LPU)
- Ministry: Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs (MoHUA) & Ministry of Panchayati Raj (MoPR)

Slide 2: Proposed Solution (Point-by-Point)
- 1. Photo Geo-Tagging & Spatial Deduplication: GPS location pin, Haversine 50m radius check prevents duplicate complaints.
- 2. 72-Hour Statutory SLA Engine: Dynamic resolution countdown (24h Immediate Danger, 48h High, 72h Medium) under Municipal Gazette guidelines.
- 3. Interactive Before & After Repair Proof Slider: Dual-layer comparison slider showing raw pothole crater vs resurfaced asphalt patch.
- 4. 1-Click RTI & CMO Legal PDF Dossier Generator: Client-side PDF petition dossier compiled via jsPDF incorporating Section 72 legal clauses.

Slide 3: System Architecture & Workflow Pipeline
- Visual 5-Stage Execution Flow: Citizen Photo Upload -> Haversine 50m Deduplication Check -> Auto-Routing to Ward Engineer/Sarpanch -> 72h Statutory SLA Countdown -> Asphalt Compaction Repair OR 1-Click RTI/CMO Legal Escalation.
- Real-World Test Region: Punjab LPU GT Road Corridor (NH-44 Phagwara & Jalandhar) + Bengaluru BBMP Grid.

Slide 4: Technology Stack & Key Libraries (Point-by-Point)
- 1. Core Framework: React 19, TypeScript 5.8, Vite 6 SPA Bundler, React Router v7 with AnimatePresence.
- 2. UI & Motion: TailwindCSS v4 (@tailwindcss/vite), Motion (Framer Motion) tubelight navbar & scroll entrance animations.
- 3. Geospatial, PDF & Data: Leaflet Maps with SVG markers, jsPDF petition compiler, Recharts analytics, LocalStorage custom hook engine (useIssues) with zero backend overhead.

Slide 5: Feasibility, Viability & Social Impact
- Economic Viability: 100% serverless static deployment with zero cloud server costs.
- Public Safety Impact: Drastically reduces two-wheeler road fatalities on high-speed GT Road corridors.
- Contractor Liability: Publicly publishes road contract IDs and tender defect liability periods to prevent corrupt re-tendering.

Slide 6: Conclusion & Live Demo
- GitHub Repository: https://github.com/Sushantcod/guddhamukt
- Team Leader: Sushant Chand (LPU)
- Tagline: Let's Make Indian Roads 100% Gaddhamukt!
```
